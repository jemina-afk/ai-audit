### STORY 1: Anthropic Launches Claude Sonnet 5 — Cheaper Agentic AI at Scale

**Source:** Anthropic / TechCrunch
**Date:** 30 June 2026

**Summary:**
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5 as its most capable mid-tier model to date, priced at an introductory rate of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through August 2026 — a significant reduction on previous-generation flagship pricing. Sonnet 5 was designed explicitly for agentic use cases: it handles browser use, terminal access, multi-step planning and tool orchestration that previously required more expensive models such as Opus. The model supports extended context windows and improved instruction-following for complex, multi-turn business workflows. Anthropic positioned the release as making production-grade agentic AI accessible to companies that previously found the cost of running autonomous agents prohibitive. It ships with full support for MCP (Model Context Protocol), allowing Sonnet 5 to plug directly into existing business tools including HubSpot, Slack, Google Workspace, Salesforce and custom databases. Early benchmarks show Sonnet 5 outperforming the previous Claude Opus 3 on coding, reasoning and multi-step task completion while costing roughly one-quarter of the price.

**Why it matters to SMEs:**
The cost barrier to AI agents has dropped substantially. Running an agent that handles 10,000 customer queries per month now costs a fraction of what it did six months ago. SMEs can realistically deploy autonomous agents for lead qualification, appointment booking, document processing and customer support without a six-figure AI budget.

**Why it matters to Tulivo Digital:**
Every AI agent Tulivo builds for clients just got significantly cheaper to run, which improves ROI calculations and shortens the payback period on implementations. This also strengthens the case for pitching agents to cost-sensitive SME clients who previously hesitated on pricing. Tulivo should update its service pricing models to reflect these new cost structures and lead with ROI in all sales conversations.

### STORY 2: OpenAI — Agent Usage Explodes 56x in Seven Months

**Source:** OpenAI
**Date:** Early July 2026

**Summary:**

The 7-month growth figures confirm that AI agent adoption has already moved past early adopter status in most industries. SMEs that have not started deploying agents are now measurably behind their competitors on productivity and response speed. **Source:** HubSpot / AIToolsReview / Concentrate NZ
**Date:** June 2026

### STORY 5: Microsoft Copilot Cowork Reaches General Availability

**Source:** Microsoft 365 Blog
**Date:** 16 June 2026

**Summary:**
Microsoft's Copilot Cowork — an agentic task execution system that accepts a defined multi-step task and autonomously completes it end-to-end, returning a finished deliverable — reached global general availability. It ships with Microsoft Purview governance controls allowing businesses to apply existing security, compliance and data residency policies directly to all Cowork-generated outputs. SMEs on Microsoft 365 Business Standard ($23.50/user/month with Copilot, now a permanent SKU) or Business Premium ($32/user/month, also now permanent) can delegate multi-step work — research and synthesis, document drafting, data analysis and reporting — to Cowork rather than managing individual steps manually. Cowork is distinct from Copilot Chat in that it does not require step-by-step prompting; users define the task once and Cowork plans and executes the required steps. Microsoft confirmed that from 1 July 2026, all Copilot-inclusive Business Standard and Business Premium SKUs become permanent pricing tiers rather than promotional, embedding AI into the SME Microsoft stack as a predictable ongoing cost. This removes the pilot-vs-permanent uncertainty that had caused some businesses to delay procurement decisions.

**Why it matters to SMEs:**
Any business on Microsoft 365 Business Standard or Premium now has access to an autonomous task agent at no additional cost. This is the fastest ROI available from any AI tool for Microsoft-dependent businesses — the licensing cost is already paid. The Purview integration also resolves the governance and data security objection that has blocked Copilot adoption in regulated SME sectors.

**Why it matters to Tulivo Digital:**
Microsoft 365 is the dominant productivity platform for UK SMEs. Tulivo should develop a Copilot Cowork Activation service: a structured onboarding engagement that maps client workflows to Cowork tasks, trains staff, and builds a library of reusable task prompts.
