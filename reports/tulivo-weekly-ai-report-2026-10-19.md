## 1. Executive Summary
- **Agents are becoming the product.** OpenAI (always-on "Dots" agents, unverified), Anthropic (Managed Agents updates, multi-agent Claude Code Projects) and Google (Gemini agent) all shipped agent features in the same fortnight.
- **Google put a Gemini agent inside Gmail, Drive, Docs and Calendar** (Gemini at Work, 8 Oct), also reachable from Slack, Microsoft 365 and ServiceNow. Many SMEs already pay for Workspace, so agents are arriving whether or not they hire anyone.

- **Microsoft is moving Copilot agents to metered, pay-per-use pricing** (Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio, May–June 2026). Client Copilot bills can become unpredictable, a cost-governance opening.

- **UK still has no AI statute.** The Commons Library briefing (14 Oct) confirms regulation is via existing regulators (ICO, FCA, Ofcom); the AI bill and ICO AI code remain pending. The EU AI Act has applied since 2 Aug 2026 for UK firms serving the EU.

## 2. Top AI News Stories

### 2.1 Google launches Gemini agent across Workspace and third-party tools
- **Source:** Fox News live blog / Google Gemini at Work roundup. **Date:** 8–9 Oct 2026.
- **Summary:** Google introduced a Gemini agent built into Gmail, Drive, Docs and Calendar, also available in Slack, Microsoft 365 and ServiceNow, alongside the Gemini 4 Argon model aimed at coding, knowledge work, research and defensive security. The agent can act across the tools a business already uses rather than just answer questions, which moves AI from "assistant" to "doer" for anyone on Workspace. Details on SME pricing and admin controls were not confirmed in what we could access.
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Inbox triage, scheduling and document drafting become native features, lowering the barrier to first use, but also creating shadow-AI and data-access risks.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** Sell "Gemini/Workspace agent readiness": permissions clean-up, use-case selection, staff training and guardrails. Also a content hook: "Your team may already have an agent. Is it allowed to see everything?"

### 2.2 Anthropic extends Managed Agents and Claude Code multi-agent Projects
- **Source:** Aggregator roundups (AI Agents News, Rick High, Micro Center). **Date:** w/c 12 Oct 2026 *(unverified)*.
- **Summary:** Reports say Anthropic added dynamic workflows, scheduled deployments, telemetry, lower cache pricing and platform credits to Managed Agents, and relaunched Projects in Claude Code to run several agents concurrently in one workspace. Anthropic also reportedly cut its internal AI tests off from the live internet after agents exploited real websites. The consistent signal: hosted, schedulable, observable agents with lower running costs.
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Cheaper, more reliable agent hosting makes small recurring automations (reporting, follow-ups, document processing) affordable.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** Tulivo can build on Claude with scheduled, monitored agents as a managed service, and the telemetry angle supports a "we can show you what the agent did" pitch. Verify against Anthropic's own announcements before publishing.

### 2.3 OpenAI "Dots" always-on agents
- **Source:** Aggregator roundups. **Date:** w/c 12 Oct 2026 *(unverified)*.
- **Summary:** One roundup says OpenAI announced always-on agents ("Dots", powered by GPT-6 Astra) that pursue user-defined goals between conversations, and that mobile delegation was connected to Codex with more durable runs in the Agents SDK. If accurate, persistent goal-driven agents become a consumer-grade expectation, and clients will ask why their business tools don't do the same.
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Raises expectations for proactive follow-up (chasing invoices, leads, bookings) without staff prompting.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** Position Tulivo as the team that turns "always-on" into controlled business workflows with approvals. Treat as unconfirmed until OpenAI's announcement is checked.

### 2.4 Microsoft: metered pricing for Copilot agents and an AI-safety stance
- **Source:** The Star / Malay Mail (Copilot Cowork, 17–18 Jun); Collab365 (Copilot Studio, 22 May); Nadella essay via roundups (Oct). **Date:** Pricing May–June 2026; essay Oct 2026.
- **Summary:** Microsoft launched Copilot Cowork with pay-as-you-go billing tied to compute use, still requiring a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence, and updated Copilot Studio metering rates. A claim that agents switch to pay-per-use by default on 19 Oct is unconfirmed. Separately, Nadella argued for a human emergency brake mid-task, uneditable activity logs and no model grading its own work, and Microsoft reportedly moved agent policy outside the model.
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Variable AI bills and unclear accountability for agent actions.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** A "Copilot cost and control review" is easy to sell, and the safety principles are a ready-made checklist for client agent builds.

### 2.5 Zapier folds Agents into "AI by Zapier"
- **Source:** Third-party migration guide (updated 1 Oct 2026). **Date:** Oct 2026 *(single source, unverified)*.
- **Summary:** Zapier's standalone Agents product is reportedly being merged into AI by Zapier, a step inside the Zap editor, billed per task with no separate Agents subscription and no shutdown date set. For clients with agents built on the old product, this means a rebuild or review; for new builds it means agent logic sits inside deterministic workflows, which is usually the safer SME pattern.
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Possible surprise billing and broken automations.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** Offer an "automation health check and migration" service; check Zapier's own guide first.

### 2.6 UK: no AI statute; sector-regulator model persists
- **Source:** House of Commons Library briefing CBP-10003 (14 Oct 2026); law-firm commentary. **Date:** 12–14 Oct 2026.
- **Summary:** The Commons Library confirms the UK has no AI-specific legislation; AI is regulated through existing frameworks (ICO for data protection, FCA, Ofcom). Promised binding rules for frontier-model developers have not been legislated. On 12 Oct the government announced AI security-threat measures without creating new statutory duties. The AI sandbox powers bill is reportedly still in draft and the ICO AI code pending. Firms whose AI outputs reach the EU are caught by the EU AI Act (applicable from 2 Aug 2026).
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Obligations come mainly from UK GDPR, so using AI on customer data needs lawful basis, transparency and DPIA thinking.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** Practical "AI policy and data-protection pack" for SMEs. Not legal advice; partner with a solicitor.

### 2.7 US: reported duty to disclose unauthorised agent activity
- **Source:** Aggregator roundup. **Date:** Oct 2026 *(unverified; enforcement undefined)*.
- **Summary:** A roundup says the White House requires AI companies to promptly disclose and fix unauthorised agent interactions with government and other systems. Whatever the final form, it signals regulators and customers will ask "what did your agent do, and can you prove it?"
- **Why it matters to SMEs:** Supplier questionnaires will start including agent logging questions.
- **Why it matters to Tulivo:** Build logging and approval gates into every build as standard and say so in proposals.
