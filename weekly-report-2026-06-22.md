### STORY 1: UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 — Critical Provisions Now Live

**Source:** ICO / CMS Law / Hill Dickinson / Employment Law Worldview
**Date:** 19 June 2026

**Summary:**
On 19 June 2026, the most consequential data protection provisions of the UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 came into force. The most significant change replaces Article 22 of UK GDPR — the rule that largely prohibited automated decision-making about individuals — with new Articles 22A to 22D. The default has effectively flipped from "not permitted unless" to "permitted provided". Businesses can now lawfully use AI to make decisions about individuals (credit scoring, job screening, customer service routing, personalisation) without obtaining explicit consent, as long as appropriate safeguards are in place. Simultaneously, a new complaints framework gives individuals the right to challenge automated decisions, and controllers must acknowledge complaints within 30 days. The ICO is also under a new statutory duty to publish a code covering both the development and use of AI. This change interacts with the EU AI Act for businesses with European customers, creating a dual compliance surface that most SMEs are entirely unprepared for.

**Why it matters to SMEs:**
Any SME using AI to screen job applicants, score leads, personalise pricing, route customer service queries or make credit-like decisions now operates under materially different rules. Those using these capabilities lawfully under the old regime may need to update their policies and safeguards. Those who avoided AI automation due to legal uncertainty may now be able to proceed — removing a major objection.

**Why it matters to Tulivo Digital:**
This is an immediate advisory opportunity. Tulivo can position itself as the bridge between AI implementation and compliance readiness — a "we build it right the first time" message. Audit services, policy template packages and compliance-ready AI workflow designs are all productisable. Sectors with highest urgency: recruitment, financial services, professional services, e-commerce.
